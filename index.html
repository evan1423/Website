About

I'm a first-year student studying FIMS and Computer Science at Western University. This site is a collection of projects I'm actively working on and refining, including web apps and AI tools. I use it as a space to build, experiment, and document what I'm learning along the way.

Most of my work happens outside of formal coursework and is driven by curiosity and problem-solving. I'm interested in understanding how systems work end to end—from front-end design and user experience to back-end logic and data handling.

The projects featured here are often iterative and evolve over time as I test new ideas, improve functionality, and refine how things work in practice. Some are exploratory, while others are longer-term builds that I continue to improve as my skills grow.

This portfolio serves both as a record of my progress and as a place to share what I'm currently developing.
